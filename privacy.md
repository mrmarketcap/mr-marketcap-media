# Privacy Policy — Mr. Marketcap

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy describes how the Mr. Marketcap automation tool ("the app") handles data. The app is a private tool used only by the owner of the Instagram account @mr_marketcap to manage that same account.

## What the app does
- Publishes the owner's own content (Reels, photos, carousels) to @mr_marketcap.
- Reads comments and insights on the owner's own posts, and helps the owner reply to them.

## Data we access
- Public profile information and media of the connected account @mr_marketcap.
- Comments on that account's posts (comment text, username, timestamp) so the owner can read and answer them.
- Account and post statistics (reach, views, likes, etc.).

## How data is used and stored
- Data is used only to operate and analyse the @mr_marketcap account.
- The app does not sell, share or transfer data to any third party.
- The app does not build profiles of individual users and does not use data for advertising.
- Access tokens are stored encrypted as secrets on the owner's own server infrastructure. Comment content is not kept in a separate database.

## Data deletion
If you commented on @mr_marketcap and want any data related to you removed, you can delete your comment on Instagram, or send a request to the contact email below. We will delete any related data within 30 days.
